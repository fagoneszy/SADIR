# Roadmap

## N0-N3

Terminal, math foundation, Braille renderer, WGS84 Earth and geodetic conversion.

## N4

Natural Earth, OSM vector ingestion, map projection, spatial indexes and terrain tile model.

## N5

OMM parser, SGP4 propagation, official validation vectors and six-digit catalog support.

## N6

Satellite overlays, Starlink constellation view, spacecraft class linkage and object inspection.

## N7

Ground tracks, observer ENU, azimuth/elevation, AOS/LOS, range, range-rate and Doppler.

## N8

UTC/TAI/TT/UT1, leap seconds, IERS EOP parser, polar motion, ERA, TEME/ITRF/GCRF transformations.

## N9

JPL Horizons normalized ephemerides, NAIF SPICE kernels, planets, Moon, natural satellites, asteroids and comets.

## N10

OBJ/STL/GLB ingestion, spacecraft wireframes, subsystem labels, public architecture profiles and model provenance.

## N11

Earth imagery, weather, seismic, fire, aurora, solar wind and event timeline.

## N12

Local interfaces, routes, neighbors, sockets, Npcap/libpcap and 5-tuple flow graph.

## N13

RIPE RIS Live BGP WebSocket stream and global routing visualization.

## N14

NDR session record/replay, binary indexes, snapshots and deterministic timeline playback.

## N15

AVX2/SIMD integration, z-buffer, depth cueing, CRT persistence and high-rate renderer.

## N16

Validation against JPL Horizons, GMAT, SPICE and SGP4 reference cases with numerical error reports.

## N17

Pluggable source adapters, credential vault integration, rate limiting and automatic schema migration.

## N18

Flight-software style documentation, ADRs, data dictionaries, operator manual and reproducible release pipeline.
