# Public Data Policy

NADIR is designed for public, legally accessible and traceable scientific/technical data.

Accepted source classes include:

- official government and intergovernmental data services
- public agency archives
- operator/manufacturer public documentation
- public regulatory filings
- public scientific archives
- open mapping and scientific datasets under their applicable terms
- authenticated official services when the user supplies their own authorized credentials

NADIR does not treat the following as valid acquisition sources:

- stolen credentials
- leaked private databases
- classified material
- access-control bypasses
- dark-web dumps of uncertain provenance
- proprietary internal engineering data obtained without authorization

The reason is technical as well as legal: unverifiable leaked material destroys provenance, reproducibility and chain-of-custody. A system intended for scientific, aerospace or government use needs auditable sources.

If a spacecraft component, exact processor, bus topology, encryption system, internal circuit, attitude algorithm, fuel load or other field is not publicly documented, the normalized value remains unknown.
